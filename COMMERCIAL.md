## Enterprise offering / 企业服务

Commercial engagement is available for organizations that need accountable delivery beyond the public repository:

- private deployment and environment hardening;
- organization-specific equipment taxonomy and classification calibration;
- private procurement knowledge bases and governed memory;
- integration with approved messaging, document, database, and business systems;
- workflow design, migration, training, and operational enablement;
- support, maintenance, service levels, and controlled upgrades;
- privacy review, data-boundary design, and acceptance documentation.

可提供以下企业服务：

- 私有化部署与运行环境加固；
- 企业专属设备分类体系和分类校准；
- 私有采购知识库及受控项目记忆；
- 经双方确认的消息、文档、数据库与业务系统集成；
- 流程设计、迁移、培训和上线辅导；
- 技术支持、维护、服务级别和受控升级；
- 隐私检查、数据边界设计与验收材料整理。

## Delivery boundary / 交付边界

The public repository is provided under Apache-2.0 on an “AS IS” basis. A commercial engagement begins only after both parties sign a separate written agreement or order that defines scope, deliverables, milestones, fees, acceptance, support, data handling, and responsibility.

公开仓库按 Apache-2.0 以“按原样”方式提供。只有双方签署书面协议或订单，明确范围、交付物、里程碑、费用、验收、支持、数据处理和责任后，商业项目才正式成立。

Unless agreed in writing, the enterprise offering does not include autonomous final classification, supplier award, contract approval, delivery acceptance, financial posting, or ERP operations. Accountable customer personnel retain approval at the applicable Gates.

除非另有书面约定，企业服务不包含由 AI 自主完成最终分类、供应商定标、合同审批、到货验收、财务记账或 ERP 操作；客户的责任人员继续在对应 Gate 做最终确认。

## What remains private / 私有内容

Customer lists, quotations, suppliers, contracts, credentials, runtime logs, organization knowledge, and calibrated rules remain outside the public repository unless the customer separately authorizes a specific disclosure in writing.

客户清单、报价、供应商、合同、凭据、运行日志、组织知识及企业校准规则默认不进入公开仓库；任何特定披露均须客户另行书面授权。
